What's The Purpose?

After earning my Bachelor's in Computer Science, I decided to move far away. Trying to find a job in this field would be a dream, but was impossible where I was at previously; it's no longer impossible now, just difficult. During my time in University, I got to learn so much about this field. There is a lot that goes into computers, and the knowledge is practically endless; with the development of AI, that progress of learning seems to have accelerated dramatically. A problem at my University (and likely others), is that the subjects being taught gave the students practically no real world examples or experiences in our classes or homework. Most of the code-alongs are built from the ground up and even those came from boilerplate tutorials professor's used years ago. So when a roadbump occured, it was difficult for the professor to solve. CS also has many logical components that can't be resolved practically (integer overflow, memory errors, etc) which are so nice to know. Just hard to prove in a real world scenerio when every company already has websites/software built years ago and have been iterated on constantly and likely will not have you mess with their big name code from the start.


Therefore, that's where this website comes in. I have created it in hopes to put myself out there and prove myself of the knowledge I have obtained, place it somewhere and iterate on it over time. It's fun, and although I do not have a programming job (yet) this site is proof to those and myself that it's possible.


So stick around for the ride! It'll be fun.
